The syllabus

What this mentorship actually covers

Not generic motivation — a working system for the specific problems that hold students back, worked through with you over calls and WhatsApp.

01

Your exam strategy

What "preparing to qualify" actually looks like week by week, and what changes once the goal shifts from clearing the exam to getting a top rank.

02

A study system that holds up

How to study effectively — sequencing topics, revision cycles, and daily routines that don't fall apart after the first tough week.

03

Boards and JEE, managed together

How to top your boards without letting JEE prep slide, and the reverse — a schedule that treats both as one plan, not two competing ones.

04

Mock test mastery

How to actually raise your mock scores — what to review after each test, which mistakes are worth fixing first, and how to test-take under time pressure.

05

The PYQ playbook

How to solve previous years' papers so they build pattern recognition, not just a completed checklist.

06

Choosing the right material

Which books, question banks, and resources are worth your time for your track — and which ones to drop so you stop spreading yourself thin.

07

Staying consistent

How to keep showing up on the weeks that don't feel motivating — the difference between a plan you make once and a habit that survives contact with a bad week.

Programs

A track for where you are right now

Mentorship is tailored by class and goal — pick the track that matches your situation.

Focus

Building the study habits and academic foundation that everything later depends on — before the board and JEE pressure starts.

For students who want to start strong instead of catching up later.

Includes

  • Fortnightly phone call check-ins
  • Daily/weekly study routine design
  • Early introduction to a competitive-exam mindset
  • WhatsApp support for doubts and planning

Focus

Topping boards while making an informed, confident choice about the stream and path after Class 10.

For students who want a strong board result without losing sight of what comes next.

Includes

  • Weekly phone call check-ins
  • Board-focused revision and PYQ planning
  • Stream and career-path guidance
  • WhatsApp support for doubts and planning

Focus

Making the jump to JEE-level rigor while boards start to matter again — the year most students lose momentum in.

For students starting JEE prep who need a plan that doesn't sacrifice boards.

Includes

  • Weekly phone call check-ins
  • Combined boards + JEE-foundation study plan
  • Study material selection for PCM
  • WhatsApp support for doubts and accountability

Focus

The highest-stakes year — managing boards and JEE at the same time, with mock scores that need to keep climbing.

For students who need a tight, realistic plan for the months before both exams.

Includes

  • Weekly phone call check-ins, more frequent closer to exams
  • Boards + JEE combined revision timetable
  • Mock test review after every attempt
  • WhatsApp support for daily doubts and course-correction

Focus

Rank-focused mentorship for JEE Main and Advanced aspirants, including droppers — built around your mock data and PYQ patterns.

For students whose main goal this year is a top JEE rank.

Includes

  • Weekly (or more frequent) phone call check-ins
  • Rank-optimization strategy from your mock and PYQ data
  • Study material audit — what to keep, what to drop
  • WhatsApp support for daily doubts and consistency
How it works

Simple to start, easy to stay consistent with

03

04

Ongoing mentorship

Regular check-in calls plus WhatsApp support for doubts, accountability, and adjusting the plan as mock scores come in.
